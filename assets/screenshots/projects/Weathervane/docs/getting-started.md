# Getting started

## What you need

- A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, and a 32 GB card
- A BME280 breakout, to begin with
- Four jumper wires

## Flash the card

Write the image to the card, add your Wi-Fi to `station.toml`, and boot.

## Your first reading

```sh
weathervane probe
weathervane read bme280
```
